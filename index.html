XYZFlow: Scaling Multidimensional Shortcut Flows for Efficient Generative Modeling

Jinxiu Liu, Xuanming Liu, Kangfu Mei, Yandong Wen, Weiyang Liu

A spatio-temporal framework for few-step generation that combines historical trajectory conditioning with Next Shortcut Prediction for stronger quality-efficiency trade-offs.

Paper PDF LaTeX Source Code (Coming soon)

Overview

XYZFlow revisits efficient generative modeling from an architectural perspective. Instead of relying only on stronger distillation objectives or larger models, it increases the expressivity of the probability flow through two coupled dimensions: temporal conditioning on denoising history and spatial autoregression over image patches.

The key mechanism, Next Shortcut Prediction, transfers the denoising trajectory of earlier patches to later ones, enabling aggressive step reduction while preserving image fidelity.

Abstract

We propose XYZFlow, a spatio-temporal framework for efficient few-step generation. XYZFlow conditions each denoising step on historical states and sequentially generates patches with trajectory-level priors from preceding patches. This multidimensional conditioning makes probability flows more structured, easier to predict, and more suitable for accelerated generation. Across model scales, XYZFlow achieves strong quality-efficiency trade-offs and remains robust under weaker teachers and across teachers of different strengths.

Key Ideas

Temporal Scaling

Condition each denoising step on the full history of previous states to straighten the reverse trajectory.

Spatial Scaling

Decompose images into patches and generate later patches using the denoising trajectories of earlier ones.

Shortcut Scheduling

Use progressive schedules such as 5→4→3→2 to reduce total denoising steps without hurting fidelity.

Highlights

36.1× Speed-up for XYZFlow-B over its teacher
1.63 FID for XYZFlow-B with GAN
1.74 FID under a weaker DiT/XL-2 teacher

The paper also shows that XYZFlow remains competitive under weaker teachers: with DiT/XL-2 (25-step) as teacher, progressive XYZFlow-B reaches 1.74 FID after GAN fine-tuning, outperforming standard 5-step distillation baselines by a large margin. Code and training recipes will be released soon.

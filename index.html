XYZFlow: Scaling Multidimensional Shortcut Flows for Efficient Generative Modeling

XYZFlow studies how to scale multidimensional shortcut flows for efficient generative modeling, with a focus on improving sample quality, training stability, and inference efficiency under progressive distillation settings.

About the Paper

This paper presents XYZFlow, a framework for scaling multidimensional shortcut flows in a way that better balances generation quality and efficiency. The core goal is to make fast generative models more practical without sacrificing the performance gains typically associated with larger or slower teacher models.

The project explores progressive training and efficient flow-based design choices, aiming to provide a clearer path toward strong few-step generation. Full technical details are available in the paper.
